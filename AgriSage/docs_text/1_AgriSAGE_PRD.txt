1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
1.1 Product Vision
Agri-SAGE is a farm decision intelligence platform — not a chatbot, not a disease detector, not a weather app. It is all of these working as one unified system: the first offline-first, simulation-grounded, evidence-transparent agricultural decision engine built for smallholder farmers in India.
Every farmer in India who cannot afford an agronomist, who has no reliable internet, who communicates in Malayalam or Tamil or Telugu, who farms 1 hectare of paddy and needs to know if the yellowing on his lower leaves is blast disease or nitrogen deficiency — and what the rain forecast means for when to spray — and whether a government scheme covers the treatment — that farmer is our user.
1.2 The Problem Being Solved
A smallholder farmer today needs to answer 20+ interacting questions to make a single crop management decision. These questions are currently fragmented across agronomists, government helplines, weather apps, WhatsApp groups, YouTube, local shops, and personal experience. Each source gives partial, often conflicting information. No single system connects them.
The cost of wrong decisions: crop loss, wasted inputs, delayed treatment, missed government support, unnecessary pesticide spend. For a family farming 2 hectares, one wrong decision in a season can mean 20-40% income loss.
1.3 The Solution
Agri-SAGE combines: multimodal input (image + voice + location + farm history) → multi-agent AI analysis (14 specialized agents) → physics-based crop simulation (DSSAT) → evidence-transparent advisory → proactive daily monitoring → government scheme matching → economic scenario comparison — delivered in regional language, offline-capable, on a Rs.8,000 Android phone.

3. PRODUCT SCOPE
3.1 In-Scope: MVP (Phase 1-2, Months 1-8)
Android application (minimum Android 8.0 / API 26)
Farmer registration via OTP (phone number only — no password)
Farm profile: location, field, crop, cultivar, planting date
Image capture with real-time quality check and retry guidance
Vision-based disease/pest/deficiency detection (5 launch crops)
Weather integration with agricultural indicator derivation
Crop stage estimation via GDD (Growing Degree Days)
Knowledge RAG: ICAR + KAU documents for 5 crops
Critic agent: cross-validation and confidence calibration
Advisory output: diagnosis + evidence + action + cost estimate + sources
Advisory history and farm observation timeline
Offline vision inference (ONNX on-device model)
Offline knowledge cache for supported crops
Malayalam language support (text + voice output)
Morning briefing: daily farm status card
Backend API (FastAPI + PostgreSQL + pgvector)
3.2 In-Scope: V2 (Phase 3-4, Months 9-18)
Government scheme matching (PMFBY, PM-KISAN, RKVY)
Simulation engine: DSSAT scenarios for rice and maize
A/B/C scenario comparison with economic analysis
Voice input in Malayalam (Bhashini STT)
Proactive risk alerts (push notifications)
Intervention tracking and outcome recording
Expert review dashboard (agronomist interface)
Tamil, Telugu, Hindi language support
Season summary report
3.3 In-Scope: V3 (Phase 5-6, Months 19-30)
Market price integration (e-NAM API)
Economics agent: full cost-profit analysis
Farmer constraint optimization (budget, labor, water)
Digital farm twin (seasonal learning and memory)
FPO / group advisory dashboard
Web application for extension workers and agronomists
Satellite imagery integration (NDVI alerts)
Multi-crop expansion (target: 15 crops)
3.4 Explicitly Out of Scope (Never Build)
Social network or community feed (misinformation risk)
Blockchain of any kind
Drone integration
Autonomous purchasing or chemical ordering
Yield guarantees or financial return promises
Custom foundation model training from scratch
50+ crops at launch (quality > quantity)
